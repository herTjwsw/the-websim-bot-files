WHAT ARE IDWR CHARACTERS?
-------------------------
IDWR characters are reusable video characters made by
Fred for the WebSim bot's video commands (/wan3,
/wan27). Every one has "IDWR" in the name - that's how
you find them. You reference the character ID and the
model uses Fred's official design. Consistent Kris in
every clip, every time.

THE MAIN THING ABOUT THEM: most IDWR characters pack
MULTIPLE DESIGNS and PROPS into ONE reference image.
The model sees the WHOLE image, so if you don't name
which design/prop you want, it may mix them together.
Always name the exact design and prop in your prompt -
that's the single most important rule for using them.
Full breakdown of every character's designs and props
is below.

HOW TO PICK A DESIGN OR PROP
----------------------------
Name the exact label from the reference in your prompt:

  "kris in the dark world design, holding the sword"
  "susie light world design with the ax"
  "spamton in his NEO form"
  "ralsei with hat"
  "berdly wielding the halberd"
  "c. round as k. round"

For props say "holding the [prop]", "wielding the
[prop]", or "no props". If you don't specify, the model
may mix designs together.


HOW TO USE THEM IN VIDEOS
-------------------------
Commands: /wan3 (best, up to 30s) and /wan27 (up to 15s).

METHOD 1 - character options (recommended)
Put the exact ID in the character slots:

  /wan3 kris and susie walk through the dark world,
        susie in her Light World design
        character1: krisidwr-kzu4
        character2: bestsusieonthisserver

wan3 supports character1 through character5, so you can
put up to 5 IDWR characters in one clip.

METHOD 2 - @mention in the prompt
Write the ID with an @ in front and it's picked up:

  /wan3 @krisidwr-kzu4 fights @spamtonidw-4iq4
        in a neon-lit street


TIPS
----
- Use the EXACT ID. "kris" alone won't work, it has to
  be krisidwr-kzu4.
- 2 characters is the sweet spot, more gets risky.
- ALWAYS name the design if the character has multiple.
- Name props explicitly, or say "no props".
- Write what they're DOING, not what they look like -
  the ref handles the design. Spend the prompt on
  action, setting, camera.
